### Case Study 01: Hyper-Local Mobile App User Acquisition (Google UAC)
- **Headline:** Slashed Mobile App Install Cost by 62% (₹32.97 → ₹12.50 CPI) via Hyper-Local Geofencing & Dayparting
- **Channel:** Google Ads (Universal App Campaigns - UAC Android/iOS, Account `520-691-9643`)
- **Key Metrics:**
  - Impressions: **10,441**
  - Clicks: **902**
  - Installs: **177+** verified downloads
  - Initial Phase (Greater Noida): ₹32.97 CPI
  - Central Noida Geofence (3km radius): ₹24.38 CPI
  - October 2026 Optimized Phase: **₹12.50 CPI** (Workers: ₹11.16 CPI, Customers: ₹15.27 CPI)
- **Technical Architecture:**
  - **Pincode Clustering:** Pincodes `201309` and `201307` generated 84% of high-intent actions.
  - **Device Elimination:** Identified and excluded tablet traffic (`₹136.52 → 0 installs`), eliminating a 10% budget leak.
  - **Dayparting Schedule:** Scheduled bids during peak conversion hours (11:00–13:00 and 17:00), dampening unprofitable early-morning clicks.
  - **Creative Grading:** 9 creative assets graded "BEST" by Google machine learning.

- **Technical Architecture:**
  - Resolved complex Meta Business Portfolio deadlock (`2604214600020812`), unblocking advertising restrictions via second-admin security protocols.
  - Implemented direct-response WhatsApp chat funnels that bypass high-friction web landing pages.

2. **Never show credit card numbers, CVVs, or full payment billing addresses** (VISA card last 4 digits are fine if framed as account setup).

4. **Mask raw personal phone numbers or direct email addresses** of private individuals in ad copy examples (keep generic business numbers or masked digits: `+91 91940 20XXX`).
5. **Never describe yourself as an "employee" or use employment contract terms**.
