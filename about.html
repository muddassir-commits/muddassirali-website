The Trajectory

From Direct Outreach to Autonomous Systems

My trajectory began on the frontline of direct sales — dialing high-volume international campaigns across healthcare and cross-border trade. That early exposure to direct human communication drilled in one irreversible principle: if it doesn't result in a closed transaction or scheduled appointment, it is noise.

"I don't teach passive theory. I engineer live campaigns, audit real developer budgets, and stress-test every automation on production traffic."

Seeking to eliminate the manual friction that hobbles modern sales teams, I immersed myself in algorithmic search buying, server-side attribution, and workflow orchestration. After achieving formal accreditations across Google Search, Apps, Measurement, and GA4, I transitioned from manual outreach to engineering autonomous lead pipelines.

Today, as Founder & Managing Director of Veloxis Global, I lead the development of high-velocity PropTech marketing architectures. Most notably, I authored the proprietary 749 Real Estate Research Study across UP & Delhi NCR, diagnosing why Indian property firms lose up to 70% of their ad spend through tracking failures and response latency.
